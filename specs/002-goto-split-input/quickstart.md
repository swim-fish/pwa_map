## 3. Smoke-test walkthrough

After a clean `npm run dev`, visit `http://localhost:5173` and:

### US1 — Pick a format, type into labelled fields

1. Click the `前往` (Go To) button at the top right.
2. The modal opens with a chip rack across the top: `自動偵測`,
   `台電座標`, `WGS84 (DD)`, `WGS84 (DMS)`, `TWD67 TM2`, `TWD97 TM2`,
   `MGRS`. The active chip is `自動偵測` and the body shows a single
   free-text field.
3. Tap `WGS84 (DD)` — the body re-lays into two labelled fields
   (`緯度`, `經度`).
4. Type `25.033611` into 緯度, `121.564472` into 經度. Press `前往`.
5. The modal closes; the map flies to Taipei 101 *without changing
   the zoom level*; a fading pin icon appears at the map center for
   ~3 s.
6. Repeat for each of the seven chips against the test vectors in
   `specs/001-coord-map-pwa/contracts/test-vectors.md` (and
   `tests/unit/fixtures/test-vectors.json`).

### US2 — Re-use a recent entry

1. After completing three Go-Tos in different formats, close and
   re-open the modal.
2. The "recents" row shows three chips, most-recent first, with a
   `goto.recent.tooltip` ("點擊使用此紀錄，長按可刪除") on hover/
   focus.
3. Tap the middle chip — the modal closes and the map flies to that
   entry; on next open, the entry is at the top.
4. Long-press another entry (≥ 500 ms) — a confirmation dialog opens.
   Confirm. The entry is removed; others are unchanged.
5. Reload the page (`Ctrl+Shift+R`). Re-open the modal. The recents
   row renders the persisted entries.

### US3 — Disambiguator + destination indicator + zoom preservation

1. Set zoom to 18 (street level) by scrolling in.
2. Click `前往`. With `自動偵測` active, paste
   `306962.887, 2769619.124` and press `前往`.
3. The disambiguator bottom sheet opens listing TWD97 zone 121
   (Taipei 101), TWD97 zone 119 (Penghu-area latitude), and TWD67
   (Taipei 101 via four-parameter).
4. Tap the TWD97 zone 121 row. The bottom sheet closes; the map flies
   to Taipei 101.
5. After landing, the zoom indicator still reads **18** — the operator's
   chosen scale is preserved.
6. The destination indicator fades in at the screen center for 3 s,
   then fades out. If you pan within those 3 s, it disappears within
   ~150 ms.
